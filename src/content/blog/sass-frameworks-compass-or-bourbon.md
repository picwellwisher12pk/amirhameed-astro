Once in a while, we see this question pop on Twitter, Reddit or StackOverflow. Pretty much anyone who has ever worked with Sass has at some point asked themselves which to choose: Compass or Bourbon? In case I’ve already lost some of you, Compass and Bourbon are frameworks for Sass. Sass, as you probably know, is a trendy CSS preprocessor, right? Okay. In the same way you put jQuery or Backbone or whatever over JavaScript, you can use a framework over Sass to help get things going. In today’s article, I’d like to give you my opinion on this topic.
