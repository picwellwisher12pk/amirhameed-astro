Facebook’s iOS apps, on the other hand, are fluid. They prioritize the design of motion; they feel like living, breathing apps. via [Faster UI Animations With Velocity.js | Smashing Magazine](http://www.smashingmagazine.com/2014/06/18/faster-ui-animations-with-velocity-js/).
