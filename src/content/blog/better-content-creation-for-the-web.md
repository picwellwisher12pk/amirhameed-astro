Ask yourself, what is one of the first things visitors look for when they reach your website? Other than a friendly interface for optimum usability, it’s the content. Content makes up the majority of your site so it is imperative that you pay attention when creating or redesigning your website. Though you learn earlier on in your career as a designer that your design can make or break your website, it should be noted that poor content can be damaging as well. In today’s article you will learn what makes good content and how to make the most out of it. via [Better Content Creation for the Web](http://www.sitepoint.com/better-content-creation-web/?utm_content=buffer5caad&utm_medium=social&utm_source=facebook.com&utm_campaign=buffer).
