Each year we award a limited number of scholarships to students of exceptional research potential who are applying for a masters by research, PhD or professional doctorate. Because scholarship numbers are limited, we conduct one highly competitive scholarship selection round each year, which we call our annual scholarship round. To be considered for a scholarship, you must apply during the annual scholarship round in September. via [QUT - Research annual scholarship round](https://www.qut.edu.au/research/scholarships-and-funding/research-scholarships?utm_source=sciencealert&utm_medium=socialmedia&utm_campaign=qut_research_scholarships).
