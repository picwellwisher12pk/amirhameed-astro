**How Big is WordPress?**

Here are just some of the facts that illustrate just how popular WordPress is:

*   WordPress has a **market share** **of 59.3%.**
    
*   Googling the term WordPress will give you **1.6 billion results**.
    
*   WordPress powers more than **17 million websites**.
    
*   Users write **69.8 million new posts** in WordPress each month.
    
*   Over **542 million words** per day are written in WordPress blogs.
    
*   The number of **page views** across WordPress blogs grew from **11.2 billion** in 2013 to **19.8 billion** in 2015.
    

**Notable WordPress Companies:**

Have you ever heard of **Automattic, Envato, and WP Engine**? These are the names of companies affiliated with the biggest CMS tool in the world.

*   **Automattic**, the parent company of WordPress, has 490 employees under its wing. Over the period of nine years, Automattic raised $190.6 million from 15 investors.
    
*   **Envato**, the company behind WordPress themes and plug-ins, is based in Australia and has 180 employees and 80 contractors. In 2015 alone, Envato earned over $400 million.
    
*   **WP Engine** is a hosting service for WordPress websites. The company has 313 employees, and it raised $40.712 million from 9 investors during 5 years.
