SolidRocks is an Autodesk™ 3dsmax™ plugin which automates the complex process of setting up rendering. It brings the best of the powerful [V-Ray™](http://www.chaosgroup.com/en/2/vray.html) rendering engine without any need to know its internal operation.

**What can SR do on this Evermotion Interior Scene ?**

* * *

How fast is SolidRocks when asked to render a difficult, complex, dark interior scene, with lots of glossies and GI detail ? **Thanks to [Evermotion](http://www.evermotion.org)** for offering this benchmark scene.

**Original Scene render** ([Archinteriors Vol 17](http://www.evermotion.org/modelshop/show_product/archinteriors-vol-17/2440/0/0/)\-Scene 5) GI : Irradiance map + Lightcache**Rendertime : 20 min 43 s****![](http://solidrocks.subburb.com/images/stories/SR097/ev_inter_orig.jpg)**

**SolidRocks render** with default settings (Quality : Ultra) GI : Irradiance map + Lightcache + **detail enhance****Rendertime : 6 min 43 s !****![](http://solidrocks.subburb.com/images/stories/SR097/ev_inter_srultra2.jpg)**  **More than 3 x FASTER !**
