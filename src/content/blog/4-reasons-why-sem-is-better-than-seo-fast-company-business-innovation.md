What SEO is justifiably famous for is its ability to convince the search engines--the algorithms that index your website--that your webpage is the most relevant one for the keywords being searched. For years, strong SEO has been every marketer's go-to tactic. But even while you've grown more adept, search engines have grown increasingly complex. These days, you can't just optimize and sit back. Search Engine Marketing (SEM)--placing ads that appear above search results--should already be in your bag of tricks. It's is more accountable, because sites like Google offer in-depth data about the performance of your ads. If you aren't already using SEM, consider making it a part of future marketing campaigns.
