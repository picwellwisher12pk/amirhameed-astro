There are dozens of popular tools to help theme developers test the quality of their code. Not only can this help reduce errors in your themes, but it also helps ensure that your theme is reliably rendered on multiple devices and browsers. Also, with WordPress performance being such a hot topic, the ability to identify potential performance issues while you’re developing your theme is extremely useful. In this article, I will explain what modern.IE is, and how it can assist you in your WordPress theme development, and provide some pointers to more information.
