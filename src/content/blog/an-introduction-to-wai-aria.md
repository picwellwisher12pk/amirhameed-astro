Consequently, the internet is no longer a place of static HTML pages, as funny as they sometimes are. Rather, it has become a playground for complex, almost desktop-like web applications, each with their own widgets, controls, and behavior. via [An Introduction to WAI-ARIA](http://www.sitepoint.com/introduction-wai-aria/).
