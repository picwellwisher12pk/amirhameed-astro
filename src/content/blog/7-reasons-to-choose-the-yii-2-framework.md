Late last year, SitePoint published an article highlighting the top PHP frameworks. Tied for the number four spot was the Yii (pronounced Yee) Framework. At that time the latest version of the framework available was 1.1.14. Recently, Yii 2.0 was made available, so you can begin to use it in production. While we did cover it recently when it was still in RC status, it just reached full release status, and we feel like it’s time to revisit the topic with some reasons for choosing it over alternatives. via [7 Reasons to Choose the Yii 2 Framework](http://www.sitepoint.com/7-reasons-choose-yii-2-framework/?utm_content=buffere5f56&utm_medium=social&utm_source=facebook.com&utm_campaign=buffer).
