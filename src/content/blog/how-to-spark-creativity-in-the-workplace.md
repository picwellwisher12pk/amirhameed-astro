Creativity isn't just for "creatives." Rather, it’s a mental tool prized by employers across all fields. Many believe it’s the key to business success, even if the word itself has become overused and blasé. If you've been feeling uninspired recently, never fear: Research shows that creativity can be learned, just like any other skill. Everyone has this potential, but not everyone takes the time and effort to transform it from a potential into a strength. via [How to Spark Creativity in the Workplace](http://mashable.com/2014/06/23/workplace-creativity/?utm_cid=mash-com-fb-main-link).
