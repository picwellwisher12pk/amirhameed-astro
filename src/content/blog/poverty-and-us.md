\[blockquote cite="Amir Hameed" type="left, center, right"\]Sitting at a table of a hotel , she was saying "Poverty should be removed from the world. Watching those poor people makes me sad. Poor people! they don't even have enough to eat. " Saying this, signs of sadness started appearing on her face. And suddenly a piece of meat fell down on floor from her hands. She looked down upon it first, called the waiter and then started eating again. Waiter got washed the floor which was just done by [Total Floor Care](http://www.totalfloorcare.biz/) and dumped the piece of meat in trash can. A silent message \[/blockquote\]
