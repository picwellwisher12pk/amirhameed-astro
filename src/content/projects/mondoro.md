### Overview
**Mondoro** is a desktop focus companion designed for deep work. It combines the proven Pomodoro timeboxing method with clock-in/clock-out time tracking, task tags, and productivity reporting.

### Key Architecture & Capabilities
- **Desktop Electron Runtime**: Native OS notifications, tray minimization, and background timer persistence.
- **Customizable Intervals**: Configurable focus sprints, short rests, and long restorative pauses with ambient sound notifications.
- **Local Persistence**: Zero cloud dependencies; all logs, session histories, and analytics remain encrypted locally on the user machine.
