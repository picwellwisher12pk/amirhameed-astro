### Overview
**ScreenCapture Pro** is a modern visual utility enabling developers and testers to capture full-page or region screenshots directly in the browser, annotate bugs with arrows, callouts, and text, and export optimized image artifacts.

### Key Architecture & Capabilities
- **Vite-Powered Development**: Near-instant HMR and ultra-lean production bundling.
- **HTML5 Canvas Drawing Engine**: Custom vector overlay layer for crisp geometric shapes, blur masking, and text callouts on captured frames.
- **Direct Clipboard & Export**: One-click PNG/JPEG compression, direct clipboard copy for Slack/GitHub, and local downloads.
