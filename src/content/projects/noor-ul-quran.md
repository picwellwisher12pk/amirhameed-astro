### Overview

**Noor-ul-Quran** is an intuitive, performance-focused Quranic study and memorization application designed for seamless daily recitation and deep textual research. Combining modern server-side rendering with local-first offline caching, it provides immediate page navigation, verse search, and high-fidelity recitation without intrusive interruptions.

---

### Core Capabilities

#### 1. Versatile Reading & Study Environments
- **Dynamic Reading Modes:** Seamlessly switch between Mushaf (Page-by-Page) mode, Continuous Flow reading, and focused Verse-by-Verse Study modes.
- **Synchronized Audio Recitations:** Instant audio streaming with playback speed control, ayah-by-ayah repeat looping for memorization, and verse highlighting.
- **Multi-Translation Comparisons:** Compare multiple scholarly English, Urdu, and international translations side-by-side.

#### 2. Personal Research & Notes Inspector
- **Multi-Notes System:** Annotate individual ayahs, categorize personal reflections, and export study notes securely.
- **Reading Resume & History:** Granular resumption system tracking exact surah, ayah, and scroll position across sessions.
- **Interactive Verse Scroll Navigator:** Ultra-smooth virtualized scrubbing and chapter navigation.

#### 3. Interactive Learning & Quiz Engine
- **Active Recall Quizzes:** Comprehensive testing engine assessing Surah order, Ayah completion, and vocabulary comprehension.
- **Gamified Progress:** Track completion milestones, streaks, and review cycles.

---

### Tech Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js 16 (Turbopack, Server Components), React 19, TypeScript
- **Cross-Platform Container:** Tauri v2 (Desktop, Android, iOS targets)
- **State Management:** Zustand with persistent storage
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS v4 with dark and light theme contrast readiness
